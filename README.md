## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **Android app developer** and software enthusiast who loves turning ideas into working things, whether that's a mobile app, a command-line tool, or a piece of hardware on my desk. I learn fast, break things often, and enjoy figuring out how stuff works under the hood.

- 🌱 Co-founder of **IoTree**, a smart plant irrigation system
- 📱 Developing **Android apps** with Kotlin
- ⚙️ Working across **C, C++ and Python** for systems and tooling
- 🔧 Tinkering with **ESP/NodeMCU** and hardware projects
- 💻 Comfortable on both **Windows and Linux**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I'm a quick learner who treats every bug as a lesson

## 🌳 Co-Founded Project: IoTree

<div align="center">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/IoTree-Smart%20Plant%20Irrigation-0a4d3a?style=for-the-badge&logo=android&logoColor=34d399" alt="IoTree" />
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Source-Private-14805e?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Closed source" />

</div>

**IoTree** is a smart plant irrigation system I co-founded and help build. It pairs custom hardware with a feature-rich Android app that keeps plants healthy and watered, over **Bluetooth or Wi-Fi**. The project is closed source, so the repository is private.

<div align="center">

| 💧 Watering | 📡 Monitoring | 🛡️ Reliability |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Automatic watering based on soil moisture | Live plant moisture readings | Redundant hardware indicators for sensors and pumps |
| Flexible watering schedules | Water level measurement | Sensor calibration from the app |
| Tank capacity measurement | Warnings about plant and water status | Stats logging for history and insights |

</div>
